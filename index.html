Feel how people move through your space before anyone walks in.

  1. Draw rooms. Drag the corners. Pull a + to bend a wall.
  2. Set the doors. Mark where people come in and where they leave. Link an exit to the next room.
  3. Guide and tempt. Arrows set the general flow. Elements pull people closer.
  4. Let them in. Choose how many, how fast and how curious. Press PLAY.

Ideas are nice. Tangible is better.
